What is a Mobius Strip?

A Möbius strip is a surface with only one continuous side and one boundary, formed by joining the ends of a strip after a half twist (180°).

Time & Space

It offers a powerful analogy for the continuity of time and space, where a path can return to its origin without ever crossing an edge, hinting at a non-linear and cyclic nature of reality.
